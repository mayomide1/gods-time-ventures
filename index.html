About Us

More Than Just A
Cyber Cafe

We're a familiar, friendly face at Lagos State University. Serving students for over 5 years and counting. Whether it's printing an assignment before it's due, making photocopies before a morning lecture, or making paymets of your school related fees without stress; we're always here to help.

Founded and run with love, our cafe was built by a LASU parent who understands the hustle of campus life and wanted to make it just a little bit easier.
